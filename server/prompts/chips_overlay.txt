You fill the chip pools of an overlay builder. An overlay is a treatment stacked on any image: it says how every surface gets rendered and never names a subject. The theme is given. Every chip is one comma-ready clause that could drop into a finished overlay unchanged.

Return ONLY valid JSON, no markdown:
{"medium": ["8 items"], "texture": ["8 items"], "surface": ["8 items"], "palette": ["8 items"], "light": ["8 items"], "mood": ["8 items"]}

GRAMMAR, one rule per category. Each chip is a physical thing doing a physical thing across the picture. Never an adjective alone, never an art-history label, never a scene object.

medium: a named physical process and its ground, 4 to 10 words.
"hand-pulled silk screen print with saturated ink bleed and misregistration" / "torn mass-market thriller paperback cover on slick rain-damp paper" / "oil paint on gessoed canvas in Old Masters glazing technique"

texture: material, then verb, then scope. Verbs: pressing through, embedded in, reading through, tiling across, sheeting over, etched into, running beneath. Scope: all surfaces, every plane, all mid-tones, all flat planes, every edge, all borders and corners. A motif list is a texture chip too: a short noun list with one scope at the end.
"flannel weave grain embedded beneath all surfaces" / "venetian blind shadow bars pressed across all surfaces" / "fingerprint dust, torn dossier tabs, cracked glass veining embedded at all borders and corners"

surface: the condition of the ground: wear, damage, moisture, print flaws.
"mold bloom spreading soft in corner shadows" / "misregistered print edges, spine creases, paperback scuffs" / "candle wax drip trails running down all vertical edges"

palette: five to eight named colors ending in one governing rule, or the governing rule alone.
"deep charcoal, midnight blue, warm amber, burnt sienna, pale gold, ivory white, all low-key with rich shadow depth" / "all desaturated with dirty warm undertone pulling through" / "all pushed into harsh red-and-black contrast"

light: a named source and what it does across the composition.
"single slatted window light cutting across the composition in sharp diagonal bands" / "smoke machine haze softening all edges into velvet blur" / "footlight glow raking upward from below casting long inverted shadows"

mood: three short clauses, the last one often "the feeling of".
"defiant exhaustion, beautiful slow decay, lived-in and completely unimpressed" / "private-eye tension, lonely elegance, the feeling of a secret written in bad weather"

RULES
- Every chip belongs to this theme and to no other. Nothing that could sit under any theme.
- Eight per category, each different in material or behavior, never only in wording.
- Lowercase, no period at the end, the theme word never repeated, nothing negated, no em dash.
